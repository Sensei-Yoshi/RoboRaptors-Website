Where your donation goes

Every dollar goes into our outreach. Here's what it pays for.

Abroad

Kits for schools

Robotics kits, laptops and lesson plans for schools without them. In Zambia this started a brand-new FLL team.

At home

Community events

Robot demos at libraries, festivals and stadiums, where kids get to drive a real competition robot.

New teams

Starting teams

Helping new FLL and FTC teams get started and coaching them through their first seasons.
