Our mission

Our story

RoboRaptors started as a community team in a basement in New Jersey. Over five FLL seasons and three FTC seasons, helping other kids get into robotics became as important to us as competing.

We still compete as FIRST Tech Challenge team #23375, and what we learn there is what we teach the teams and students we work with.
