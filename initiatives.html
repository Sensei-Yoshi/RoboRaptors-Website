Outreach initiatives

We're more than a robotics team. We bring robotics to kids who haven't had the chance to try it, in New Jersey and abroad.

Our reach

We've reached 9,000+ people at community events, helped start or mentor 7 FIRST teams, and started international projects in 2 countries.

Programs

Three ongoing programs, all planned and run by our students.

5Kits sent abroad

Global STEM access

We send robotics kits, laptops and lesson plans to schools that don't have them. Our first project in Zambia led to a brand-new FLL team, and we've since started a second project in Libya.

Community outreach

We take our competition robot to libraries, festivals and stadiums and let kids drive it. Then we talk with families about how to join a FIRST team.

Community events
7Teams founded or mentored

Starting new teams

We founded two FLL teams, RoBros and Cosmic Goosling, and coached three FTC teams (Exsurgo Nova, Byte Battalion and Pancake Annihilators) on autonomous programming. Robo Rovers, an FLL team we worked closely with, won the NJ State Championship Award.

Featured project

The Zambia project

We sent robotics kits and a lesson plan to Hillcrest Boys School, and the students started their own FLL team.

One of our mentors met students there who wanted to learn robotics but had no equipment. Our members collected the kits and wrote a lesson plan covering robotics basics, programming and FIRST competitions. The students went on to host an FLL event of their own.
